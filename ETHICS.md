# 私隱與倫理｜Ethics and privacy

## 私隱優先｜Privacy first

聲音即使冇講姓名，仍然可能識別到一個人。本展示因此只公開合成測試文字、人工核對後嘅去識別化結果、aggregate metrics、圖表同示範程式。

Voice can identify a person even when no name is spoken. This showcase therefore contains only synthetic test text, reviewed de-identified outcomes, aggregate metrics, charts and demonstration code.

## 資料邊界｜Data boundary

| 不會公開 Not published | 原因 Reason |
|---|---|
| 原始或處理後錄音 / Raw or prepared audio | 降低身份識別及 voice-cloning 風險 / Reduce identification and voice-cloning risk |
| 服務憑證及可識別執行資料 / Service credentials and identifiable execution data | 保護存取權限及參與者私隱 / Protect access and participant privacy |
| 完整原始輸出資料集 / Complete raw-output dataset | 避免重新識別及不必要披露 / Avoid re-identification and unnecessary disclosure |
| 客戶、保單持有人或真實 claim 資料 / Customer, policyholder or real claim data | 本展示只使用合成情境 / The demonstration uses synthetic scenarios only |

## 人手監督｜Human oversight

高風險內容必須確認、澄清或轉介人手。系統唔會提供保險建議、產品推薦、承保決定、理賠決定或賠償承諾。

Every high-risk output requires confirmation, clarification or human review. The system does not provide insurance advice, product recommendations, underwriting decisions, claim decisions or payment promises.

## 管治對照｜Governance alignment

設計採用風險為本、人手監督及資料最少化方法，並參考香港個人資料私隱專員公署嘅 [Artificial Intelligence: Model Personal Data Protection Framework](https://www.pcpd.org.hk/english/resources_centre/publications/files/ai_protection_framework.pdf)。

The design uses a risk-based, human-oversight and data-minimisation approach informed by the Hong Kong PCPD’s [Artificial Intelligence: Model Personal Data Protection Framework](https://www.pcpd.org.hk/english/resources_centre/publications/files/ai_protection_framework.pdf).

呢個係工程控制對照，唔係法律意見，亦唔代表已證明符合所有 PDPO 或行業要求。This is an engineering-control mapping, not legal advice or proof of compliance with every PDPO or industry requirement.

## 公平性限制｜Fairness limitation

一位 speaker 唔足以衡量公平性，因此本展示唔會作出整體粵語、全港或任何人口群組表現聲明。

One speaker is insufficient to measure fairness, so the showcase makes no Cantonese-wide, Hong Kong-wide or demographic performance claim.
