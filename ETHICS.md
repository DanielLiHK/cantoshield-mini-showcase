# 私隱與倫理｜Ethics and privacy

## Privacy first

聲音即使冇姓名，仍可能識別一個人。本展示因此只公開合成測試文字、人工核對後嘅去識別化結果、aggregate metrics、圖表同示範程式。

Voice can identify a person even without a spoken name. This showcase therefore contains synthetic test text, reviewed de-identified outcomes, aggregate metrics, charts and demonstration code only.

## 冇公開嘅資料｜Not published

- 原始或處理後錄音 / raw or prepared audio;
- 服務憑證、private configuration 或可識別執行資料;
- 完整 raw transcript dataset 或私有研究紀錄;
- 客戶、保單持有人或真實 claim 資料。

## Human oversight

高風險內容必須確認、澄清或轉介人手。系統唔會提供保險建議、產品推薦、承保決定、理賠決定或賠償承諾。

Every high-risk output requires confirmation, clarification or human review. The system does not provide advice, recommendations, underwriting, claim decisions or payment promises.

## Governance alignment

The design uses a risk-based, human-oversight and data-minimisation approach consistent with the Hong Kong PCPD’s [Artificial Intelligence: Model Personal Data Protection Framework](https://www.pcpd.org.hk/english/resources_centre/publications/files/ai_protection_framework.pdf).

呢個係工程控制對照，唔係法律意見，亦唔代表已證明符合所有 PDPO 或行業要求。

## Fairness limitation

一位 speaker 唔足以衡量公平性。No Cantonese-wide, Hong Kong-wide or demographic performance claim is made.
